# Platform specs (confirm on the platform's help page before a paid campaign)

| Placement | Format | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels, Stories, Shorts, TikTok | `reel` | 1080x1920 | top 12% and bottom 22% are covered by UI; the engine keeps text in the safe area |
| Instagram/Facebook feed ads | `portrait` | 1080x1350 | best default for ads |
| Square feed, LinkedIn | `square` | 1080x1080 | autoplays muted: use `say` captions |
| LinkedIn, YouTube, website | `landscape` | 1920x1080 | `landscape4k` for 3840x2160 |
Output: H.264, yuv420p, BT.709, 30 fps, AAC 192k, about -14 LUFS. Upload the MP4, never a screen recording of a preview.
